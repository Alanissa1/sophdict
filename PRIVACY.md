Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

SophDict is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle data through our use of third-party services.

1. Information Collection and Use
   We do not require user accounts or collect personal identifying information (PII) directly. However, to provide our dictionary and translation services, we use the following infrastructure:

• Vercel: Our application is hosted on Vercel. Vercel collects standard web server logs (such as IP addresses and browser types) to ensure the security and performance of our website.
• Vercel Analytics & Speed Insights: We use these tools to measure anonymous traffic and performance metrics to improve the user experience.
• Upstash Redis: We use Upstash to store word caches and search frequencies anonymously. This data is used to improve search speed and provide suggestions. No personal user data is linked to these records.

2. Third-Party Services
   When you perform a search or translation, data is processed by:
   • Merriam-Webster API: For dictionary and thesaurus data.
   • Microsoft Azure Translator: For text translation services.
   • Datamuse API: For search suggestions.

These services receive the text you enter but do not receive your personal identity from SophDict.

3. Cookies and Local Storage
   SophDict uses browser `localStorage` to save your preferences, favorites, and search history locally on your device. This data stays on your device and is not shared with us.

4. Contact Us
   If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: info@sophdict.com
